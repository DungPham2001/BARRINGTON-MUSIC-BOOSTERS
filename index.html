About Us

A community that plays its part

Barrington Music Boosters, Inc. is a nonprofit booster organization based in Lithia, Florida. Our core area of activity is supporting music education, and specifically accompanying the music program at Barrington Middle School.

We bring together families, educators and neighbors who believe that music belongs in every student's school day, because it builds confidence, teamwork and creativity that last a lifetime.

Our Mission

Why music matters

We exist to make sure the Barrington Middle School music program has the community behind it to learn, perform and grow.

01

Creativity

Music gives students a way to express ideas and discover talents they did not know they had.

02

Teamwork

Ensembles teach listening, discipline and the joy of creating something bigger together.

03

Confidence

Performing on stage helps young musicians grow into confident, resilient learners.

What We Do

Standing beside the music program

Our work is all about helping Barrington Middle School musicians succeed.

Support the Program

We accompany the school's music program and help it have what it needs for students to learn and play.

Celebrate Performances

We cheer on students at concerts and events, and help bring families together to share in the music.

Build Community

We connect parents, teachers and neighbors who want to give back to local music education.
